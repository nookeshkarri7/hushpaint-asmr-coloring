About Hushpaint

Hushpaint is a coloring app for Android made for winding down. Each tool has its own quiet sound, every stroke saves itself, and no full-screen ad ever interrupts a picture.

Get it on Google Play Watch the trailer

What's inside

  • Tap-to-fill coloring plus marker, crayon and spray for free drawing that stays under the lines.
  • 72 original pages in four sets (Flowers, Mandalas, Cozy, Nature) and a fresh page every day.
  • Coins from coloring, a 7-day check-in and finished pages. Spend them on hints and new pages. Nothing is for sale.
  • Works offline. Paintings stay on your phone, with undo and autosave.
  • Light and dark themes, no flashing effects, reduce-motion respected.
